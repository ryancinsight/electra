# Electrical design evidence for v1

Reviewed through 2026-11-27. The [backlog](../backlog.md) assigns implementation to releases. The [checklist](checklist.md) defines the tests. Vendor documentation establishes reference workflows. Published research provides hypotheses and independent test designs. Neither establishes Electra capability until its fixture passes.

## Reference workflows

| Reference | Documented workflow | Electra disposition and acceptance fixture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OrCAD X / PSpice | Capture and board layout share electrical constraints. PSpice supports worst-case and Monte Carlo analysis from schematics. [Constraint Manager](https://resources.academic.cadence.com/schematic-capture/managing-constraints-like-a-pro-in-orcad-x-faq) · [PSpice FAQ](https://www.cadence.com/en_US/home/tools/pcb-design-and-analysis/orcad/faqs.html) | 0.5 and 0.7. Rank a divider’s resistor sensitivities, reproduce its worst-case extrema and compare seeded yield to an independent distribution. Edit a spacing rule in the schematic, find the same rule ID and violation on the board, then clear it in both views. |
| FOSSEE eSim | Its model/subcircuit builders, mixed-signal flow and node/branch plotting connect reusable models to simulation. The project distributes NgVeri and NGHDL tooling. [Official resources](https://esim.fossee.in/resources) · [Downloads](https://esim.fossee.in/downloads/) · [Plotting workflow](https://esim.fossee.in/) | 0.2, 0.5 and 0.6. Author a parameterized model, instantiate it twice, and drive an analog RC stage from a documented digital model subset. Verify logic transition order, analog threshold time, probe direction and save/reopen. Full Verilog/VHDL language compatibility is deferred pending a separate language-coverage plan. |
| EasyEDA Pro | Schematic/PCB reuse blocks can be revised, and layout templates can transfer placement and routing. PCB-to-schematic updates cover selected component attributes. [Reuse blocks](https://prodocs.easyeda.com/en/reuse-block/edit-update-reuse-block/) · [Placement and routing templates](https://prodocs.easyeda.com/en/pcb/design-update-place-and-route-from-reuse-template/index.html) · [Reverse updates](https://prodocs.easyeda.com/en/pcb/design-update-pcb-to-schematic/) | 0.6–0.7. Instantiate two channels from one versioned block. Apply an ECO to one instance, preview affected nets and pads, and confirm unrelated references, routing and locked overrides persist. Reverse-annotate a reference designator and retain undo history. |
| DipTrace | Hierarchical capture, interactive differential-pair routing, length-tolerance checks, 3D board preview and STEP export. [Schematic and 3D overview](https://www.diptrace.com/diptrace-software/) · [Layout and routing](https://www.diptrace.com/diptrace-software/pcb-layout/) | 0.7 and 0.9. Route and tune a pair to an Aequitas length/skew constraint. Violate the budget by a known amount and check readback. Verify board thickness, connector height/orientation and mechanical coordinates in an independently read STEP assembly. |
| KiCad | Documented symbol, footprint, schematic and board formats, plus schematic-linked operating point, DC, AC and transient simulation. [File formats](https://dev-docs.kicad.org/en/file-formats/) · [Simulator](https://www.kicad.org/discover/spice/) | 0.2–0.7. Pin supported format versions and round-trip symbols, pad mappings, node identities and component models. Run the same deck through the headless analyzer and native workbench; compare numerical data. |

These selections target the v1 workflow. Native proprietary project import, complete HDL language coverage and cloud co-editing each need a separate fixture, compatibility scope and release decision. Competitor executables and SDKs are optional reference tools.

## Research decisions

| Evidence and maturity | What the study tests, and its limit | Electra decision and independent check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mixed-signal simulation methods**, *Electronics*, 2026. Peer-reviewed review. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics15081687) | Compares solver methods across accuracy, throughput, capacity, convergence and scaling. Aggregated studies use different models and workloads. | 0.4–0.5. Compare adaptive time integration and event synchronization on the same rectifier and ADC-threshold fixtures. Publish waveform error, missed events, failure count, wall time and memory on a fixed machine, including failed runs. |
| **ADSPICE**, November 17, 2026. arXiv preprint. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23571) | Adds automatic differentiation to a SPICE workflow and compares gradients and optimization cost on selected circuits. Its scaling results depend on the chosen circuits and reference solvers. | 0.5 experiment. Evaluate a typed sensitivity or adjoint path with Coeus/Athena. Compare divider derivatives to closed form and smooth biased circuits to central differences over a step-size sweep. Report solve counts and absolute error near zero derivatives. Retain finite differences as an independent oracle. |
| **Modern Automatic PCB Placement With Complex Constraints**, *IEEE TCAD*, May 2026 issue. Peer-reviewed primary study. [Publication](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11195800) · [Author institution](https://scholars.lib.ntu.edu.tw/entities/publication/0793e75a-6b1c-4ab8-b794-2451abb8ae9c) | Combines pad alignment, flow-guided placement and legalization on industrial layouts. Reported routability improvements depend on the benchmark and routing configuration. | 0.7 experiment. Compare against the existing driver placer using the same netlist, router, seeds and wall-time budget. Report final routed nets, open/short count, rule violations, via count and length over repeated and held-out boards. |
| **PCB-Bench**, ICLR 2026. Peer-reviewed benchmark. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/051f3997af1dd65da8e14397b6a72f8e-Abstract-Conference.html) | Tests design comprehension across questions, images and complete board projects. A correct answer to a question does not establish a legal route. | 0.6–0.7. Where permissions allow, use held-out projects for multi-sheet cross-probing and pad-to-net identity. Measure those against connectivity truth. Route legality still requires electrical checks and DRC. |
| **PCBSchemaGen**, 2026. arXiv preprint version reviewed. [Manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00510) | Uses package and pin constraints to verify proposed schematics. A structurally valid design still needs operating-point, bandwidth and tolerance checks. | 0.6. Inject swapped package pins, missing power and wrong model-pin ordering into a reference library. Report each correct diagnostic and false positive; independently simulate the corrected circuit. Generated designs remain experimental. |
| **Via and capacitor co-design for PDN impedance**, *Electronics*, 2026. Peer-reviewed primary study. [Paper and measurement results](https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics15163523) | Optimizes via locations and capacitor selections using a precomputed inductance model and compares results with measurements. A new stack-up requires its own extraction and validation. | 0.8. Compare a fixed-via baseline with joint via/decap choices at equal part budget. Report magnitude versus frequency, largest target exceedance, resonance and BOM count. Recompute the selected layout independently. Qualify measured claims only over a calibrated band. |
| **Passivity enforcement of S-parameter rational models**, *IEEE Transactions on Electromagnetic Compatibility*, 2025. Peer-reviewed primary study. [Publication](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11018168) | Perturbs residues to enforce passivity of rational scattering models. Repair can alter fit quality. | 0.8. Inject a passivity violation into a known two-port. Verify detection, rerun a bandwide passivity test after repair, report complex fit error on held-out frequencies and check time-domain energy with passive terminations. |
| **Layout-dependent thermal performance of PCB-embedded resistive heating elements**, *Power Electronic Devices and Components*, 2026. Peer-reviewed primary study. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pedc.2026.100136) | Compares thermal simulations and experiments on PCB heater layouts. Copper thickness changes heat spreading. Board construction and thermal boundary conditions limit transfer to another design. | 0.8. Record electrical input power, stack-up and ambient conditions for a two-layout coupon. Compare the peak and spatial temperature rise to measured locations with uncertainty. Report absolute temperature and temperature rise as separate Aequitas quantities. The November 28 scope decision also accepts an independent simulator replay of both published layouts at stated boundary conditions. Numerical agreement qualifies the solver comparison. Physical accuracy still requires measurement uncertainty and registration. [Heater results](field/heater-observations.md) |

## Keeping the evidence current

The initial scan covered work dated 2025 through 2026-11-24. Product sources were the vendors’ documentation and FOSSEE’s official site. Literature searches combined “mixed-signal simulation”, “SPICE sensitivity”, “PCB placement and routability”, “schematic verification”, “via and decap PDN optimization”, “passivity enforcement” and “PCB electrothermal experimental validation” with publication years. Candidate results were checked against proceedings, journals, author repositories or the identified preprint.

At each minor release and before the 1.0 RC, search from the previous review date through the new cutoff. Search IEEE TCAD, TEMC and TPEL, DAC, ICCAD, DATE, relevant circuit- and board-design journals, and arXiv. Cover circuit/DAE solvers, compact and behavioral models, mixed-signal timing, optimization, routing, SI/PI, thermal coupling, visualization and fabrication verification. Include relevant negative results and non-ML methods. Record date window, search terms, DOI or stable URL, peer-review status, dataset/model availability and the tested operating regime.

For each relevant result, assign an owner, comparison baseline, metric, limitation and adopt/experiment/defer decision. Link a release gate before promoting an experiment. A speedup, accuracy figure or manufacturing result transfers to Electra only after an independent replay. The register is a dated, scoped review; new evidence remains eligible at every release.

### 0.6 evidence refresh

The November 25–27, 2026 incremental search covered IEEE Xplore, ACM and arXiv using schematic simulation, DAE, SPICE, behavioral and mixed-signal models, probing, routing optimization, SI/PI, electrothermal analysis and fabrication verification. No newly dated result screened in this window supplied a new acceptance fixture. The prior eSim, EasyEDA and DipTrace workflow comparisons remain the basis for the two-sheet qualification. It exercises a Windows host, the independent KiCad 9.0.2 property round-trip and a resistive DC project. The [milestone checklist](checklist.md#v06) links the executable evidence.

### 0.7 evidence refresh

Reviewed November 27, 2026. The search repeated the previous cutoff for PCB placement, pad alignment, routing and differential-pair verification across TCAD, DAC, ICCAD, DATE and arXiv, with adjacent searches for SPICE/DAE, compact and behavioral models, SI/PI and electrothermal verification. The prior TCAD placement study supplied the experiment design. The [held-out comparison](board/placement.md) uses independent KiCad layouts, two fixed seeds, a common router, physical DRC and process measurements. All eight configurations produced faults and the experimental policy routed fewer nets at both seeds. Retain the driver baseline and leave the experimental policy opt-in. The [four-layer fixture](board/editor.md) independently qualifies layout reuse, ECO, physical rules, signed margins and interchange.
