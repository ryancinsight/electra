# Passivity repair for delayed reciprocal fits

Electra represents the qualified board interconnect fit as a causal common delay followed by a real symmetric reciprocal two-port. In even and odd power-wave coordinates its scattering matrix is

`S(f) = exp(-j 2 pi f tau) Q diag(g_even, g_odd) Q^T`,

where `Q` is the orthonormal sum/difference transform. Power-wave network theory makes passivity equivalent to `S* S <= I`. The delay has unit magnitude and `Q` preserves Euclidean power, so the two singular values are `|g_even|` and `|g_odd|` at every finite frequency. `DelayedReciprocalModel::project_passive` clamps those two modal gains to `[-1, 1]`; it therefore repairs the complete model, not only sampled frequencies. This statement does not extend to an arbitrary rational fit.

The qualification fixture uses a 0.75 ns delay and an independent reference with reflection 0.02 and transmission 0.63. It fits modal gains on 20, 80, 160 and 320 MHz samples. The disjoint held-out set is 37, 113, 241 and 419 MHz. Perturbing the fitted gains by +0.01 and -0.47 injects an odd-mode gain of -1.08, a largest singular value of 1.08 and a -0.08 passivity margin. Projection changes that gain to -1.00. Across the held-out set, the complex RMS error over reflection and transmission changes from 0.23505318547 to 0.19506409203, a reduction of 0.03998909344. A separate 1 MHz-grid sweep checks the repaired model from 1 through 500 MHz; the modal derivation above supplies the stronger all-frequency result.

An independent 0.25 ns-step travelling-wave recurrence takes the repaired zero-frequency coefficients `r = -0.17` and `t = 0.83`, then propagates incident and reflected waves without frequency-domain evaluation. It sums model loss separately in even and odd coordinates as `(1 - g²)|a_mode|² dt`. At a 50 ohm reference, resistive loads of 172.22222222 ohm and 18.96551724 ohm have reflections +0.55 and -0.45. Source energies of 0.20656811510 nJ and 0.21860779245 nJ divide into load absorption of 0.12118637912 nJ and 0.13815797249 nJ plus model dissipation of 0.08538173598 nJ and 0.08044981996 nJ. The balance includes the finite record's geometrically bounded in-flight tail and closes within a 2,048-operation rounding bound of 1.14e-22 J. The opposite reflection signs also produce opposite second-return phases, exercising distinct termination impedances rather than repeating one magnitude-only case. An odd-mode pulse through the unprojected fit produces negative dissipation, so the transient oracle detects the injected active mode rather than accepting any fit with plausible forward energy.
